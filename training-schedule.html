BARBELL TRAINING SCHEDULES

Your next lift stays next.

Squat, bench, deadlift, press: four lifts in order. Choose the days you can train, then continue the sequence on your next gym day. There is no need to squeeze all four into one calendar week.

TWO DAYS A WEEK

A simple Monday and Thursday example.

With one main lift per session, each group of four sessions takes two calendar weeks. The program's next week begins after you finish all four lifts.

THREE DAYS A WEEK

Let the rotation cross Friday.

On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, the fourth lift moves to the following Monday. The order stays intact instead of dropping a lift at the end of the week.

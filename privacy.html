Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Training data

Liftgrove stores the profile, equipment, workouts, schedule, and training history you enter on your device. No Liftgrove account is required. The current app does not send this data to us, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Rest reminders

If you allow notifications, Liftgrove schedules rest-timer reminders on your device. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Exports and backups

When you export workout CSV or a backup, you choose where the file goes using iOS sharing or Files. Backups can contain your training history and settings; keep them somewhere you trust. Files you export remain where you saved them until you remove them.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Liftgrove Pro purchase. The app checks your purchase entitlement with StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include so we can answer you. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive health information.

Deleting data and changes

Removing Liftgrove from your device removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. Delete exported backups separately from their saved locations. We will update this page if Liftgrove’s data practices change.

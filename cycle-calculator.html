HOW THE PREVIEW WORKS

The weights, before the workout.

The classic main sets use 65%, 75%, and 85% of your training max in week 1; 70%, 80%, and 90% in week 2; and 75%, 85%, and 95% in week 3. The last working set allows extra reps. Week 4 uses 40%, 50%, and 60% for a lighter deload. Choose 5s Pro to use five reps for every working set without an extra-rep target.

Weeks 1–3 also begin with 40% × 5, 50% × 5, and 60% × 3 warm-ups. A warm-up cannot be lighter than your selected bar, and equal warm-up loads appear once. Weights round to your chosen increment. This preview uses the same standard set rules as Liftgrove; the app can also plan your four lifts, account for your actual plates, log sets, and carry training-max decisions into the next cycle.

This is a planning estimate, not a claim about what you can safely lift. Choose a training max that fits your current ability.

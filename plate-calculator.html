THE MATH, MADE SIMPLE

What plates go on each side?

Subtract the bar weight from your target, then divide by two. Load the same plates on both sides.

57.5 kg target

With a 20 kg bar, each side needs 18.75 kg.

15 kg + 2.5 kg + 1.25 kg per side

225 lb target

With a 45 lb bar, each side needs 90 lb.

45 lb + 45 lb per side

Available plates vary by gym. Liftgrove uses your bar and plate inventory, and shows a nearby load when the exact target cannot be made.

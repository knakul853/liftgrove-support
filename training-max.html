HOW THIS CALCULATOR WORKS

A simple estimate, then room to grow.

For a set of two to 30 reps, the estimated one-rep max is the weight lifted × (1 + reps ÷ 30). A one-rep lift uses the weight itself. The training max is 85% or 90% of that estimate, rounded to your chosen increment. This is the same starting calculation Liftgrove uses; inside the app you can also enter a known training max and confirm later changes yourself.

100 kg × 5 reps

Estimated max: about 116.7 kg.

At 90%, rounded to 2.5 kg: 105 kg training max.

225 lb × 3 reps

Estimated max: 247.5 lb.

At 90%, rounded to 5 lb: 225 lb training max.

Rep-based maxes are estimates. Use the number as a planning starting point and adjust it to your training, rather than treating it as a promise of what you can lift.

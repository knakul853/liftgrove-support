Email support

Where is my training saved?

Your workouts are saved on your device. Use Settings → Create backup before changing devices or removing the app, then use Restore backup on the new installation.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings → Liftgrove Pro → Restore purchase using the same Apple Account that bought it. If it doesn’t unlock, email us with the app version and what you see. Please don’t send passwords or payment details.

Can I change units or plates?

Yes. Open Settings to choose kilograms or pounds and to adjust your bar and plate inventory. Check your planned weights after changing equipment.
